<div align="center">

<h1>🩺 About MediCare</h1>

<p>
  <strong>MediCare</strong> is a desktop-based
  <strong>Doctor Consultation & Appointment Management System</strong>
  developed using Java Swing, JDBC and MySQL.
</p>

<p>
  The project is designed to simplify the process of discovering doctors,
  booking appointments and managing consultation records through a
  centralized digital system.
</p>

</div>

<br>

<table>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h2>🎯 The Idea</h2>

<p>
Traditional appointment management often depends on paper records,
manual registration and repeated communication between patients and clinics.
</p>

<p>
<strong>MediCare</strong> aims to transform that process into a simple
digital workflow where patients and doctors can interact with the system
through dedicated interfaces.
</p>

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

<h2>💡 The Goal</h2>

<p>
The goal of MediCare is not just to create another CRUD application.
It is an attempt to understand how different software concepts come
together to form a complete real-world application.
</p>

<p>
From the user interface to the database, every layer works together
to create a complete appointment management experience.
</p>

</td>

</tr>
</table>
